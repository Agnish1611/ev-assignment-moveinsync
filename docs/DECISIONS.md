| 2026-10-30 | D20: A charge stop is inserted only after a direct append fails on energy or reserve. | 2026-10-30 | D21: The flow re-timer runs once after construction and once on the ALNS incumbent. Trip assignments stay frozen. | 2026-10-30 | D22: A battery or late-cab drift unlocks only that cab's failing trips inside the lock window.
